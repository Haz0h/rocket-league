History of Rocket League

Released: July 7, 2015 by Psyonix.

Originally planned as a sequel to an earlier game, Rocket League quickly became a global phenomenon and one of the biggest esports titles.

First Major Updates: Early updates added new game modes, arenas, and ranked seasons. The Rocket Pass system (introduced in 2018) significantly increased long-term player engagement.

Player Numbers:

  • Over 90 million registered players worldwide at its peak
  • Millions of concurrent players across all platforms
  • Still maintains a strong player base in 2026

Rocket League started as a small indie title and grew into a global esport with massive tournaments and a passionate community.

What is Rocket League?

Rocket League is a fast-paced game that combines soccer with rocket-powered cars. You score goals by hitting a giant ball with your car.

It may look simple, but it has incredible depth — flying, boosting, aerial shots, and endless skill expression.

The best part? The game is completely free to play on Steam, Epic Games, PlayStation, and Xbox.
